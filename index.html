How I work

Honest from the start

I tell you early what is likely to be supported, what isn't, and what that means for time and cost. I can't promise an outcome, because Council makes the decision, but you won't be guessing.

Open with Council

Clear, early conversations and well-organised applications cut down the back-and-forth that slows approvals. I treat Council officers as people to work with, not obstacles.

Plain English

Advice and reports you can read, understand and use to make decisions, without the jargon.

Why all three matter

Good scoping, a solid planning report and open communication each help on their own. Together they compound. Move the sliders and watch the path to a decision.
