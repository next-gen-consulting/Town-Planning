Planning from the Council side, now working for you.

Hi, I'm Taj Gieskens. I spent five years working inside local government before starting Next Generation Town Planning.

In that time I saw what makes applications move smoothly and what makes them stall. Most delays aren't due to the merits of a proposal. They came from unclear communication, missing information and surprises late in the process caused by a lack of due diligence or honesty.

I started this practice to do it differently: be honest with clients about where they stand, and keep a clear, open line with Council from first conversation through to the decision.
